aperture or focus-by-wire — which is exactly why it's popular for wide-field
astrophotography, but also why it needs extra hardware around it to be
usable on a tracking mount: a way to mount it securely to a dovetail or
ball head, and a way to nail focus precisely on stars, where the stock
focus ring's throw and grip make fine adjustment difficult.
