## Overview

A 3D-printed, cylindrical camera housing built around a wide-angle lens
module, aimed straight up to capture the whole sky in one frame. The lens
sits behind a clear printed dome, bolted through the front flange into
the body with four machine screws — sealing the optics off from the
weather while keeping the full field of view unobstructed. A cooling fan
is mounted at the rear of the housing, wired alongside the camera's power
and data leads, to keep the electronics from overheating during long
overnight capture sessions.
